The problem

Every business leaks in three ways.

You built this on vision and sheer will. But most businesses run on sticky notes, memory, and heroic effort — and that leaks. Quietly. Daily.

Money

The call that rang while everyone was busy. The quote nobody followed up. The work that was never invoiced. The customer who drifted away.

Time

The same info typed into three places. The same question answered forty times a week. You doing $20-an-hour tasks between $500-an-hour decisions.

Quality

Steps that live in one person's head. Work done differently every time. Mistakes found by the customer instead of the process.

We build simple, integrated systems — so everything flows into and out of one place.

The map

Every business is the same machine.

A bakery, a plumbing company, a law office — same machine, different uniform. Work flows through five stages, and the leaks live at the handoffs between them. Many of the steps themselves are repetitive and automatable — and when we automate the repetitive and monotonous, humans get to do what they do best: create and oversee.
