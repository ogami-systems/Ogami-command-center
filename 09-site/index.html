Why businesses leak

You built it on vision and sheer will. It runs on sticky notes and memory.

The traits that make someone good at starting a business are almost the opposite of the traits it takes to run one profitably. Most owners never get an operations person — so the business runs on a patchwork of memory, text threads, and heroic effort. It works well enough to survive. But it leaks everywhere: quietly, daily, in ways the owner has learned to stop seeing.

That is our job. We see what you've stopped seeing, measure what it costs, and fix it.

Money

Missed calls that were jobs. Quotes that never got a follow-up. Work never invoiced, invoices never chased, customers who drifted away because nobody stayed in touch.

Time

The same information typed into three places. Questions answered forty times a week. Paperwork nights. The owner doing $20-an-hour tasks between $500-an-hour decisions.

Quality

Steps that live in one person's head. Work done differently every time. Mistakes discovered by the customer instead of the process.

What we do about it

Diagnose. Seal. Maintain.

01

Diagnose

The Ogami Operating Audit — a structured, expert walk through your entire business, end to end. Every area scored, every leak measured in dollars and hours. You leave with a written report you can act on, with or without us.

02

Seal

Each leak gets sealed with the right instrument: automated systems that do the task themselves, a better process for doing it — often both. The biggest, fastest-payoff leak goes first, so the work proves itself before it asks for anything more.

03

Maintain

Sealed systems get watched. Every month you get a simple report: what the systems caught, what they recovered, what they saved, and where the next leak is. The results make the case — we never have to.

Our principles

How we work, in five rules.

Diagnosis before prescription.

We never recommend a fix before the leak is found, measured, and shown to you. Tools are prescriptions, not products.

Show it, never pitch it.

Every month, the report makes the case with numbers. If the numbers ever stop making the case, you should stop paying us. No long-term contracts — the results are the contract.

One leak at a time.

Businesses don't need a transformation project. They need the biggest hole sealed, then the next one. Momentum beats overhaul.

Simple over impressive.

The best fix is the simplest thing that permanently closes the leak. Sometimes that's software. Sometimes it's a checklist and a rule.

The owner's life is the metric.

Revenue recovered and hours returned only matter if you get your evenings, your focus, and your business back.
