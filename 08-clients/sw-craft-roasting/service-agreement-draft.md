## 1. What Ogami is building

One system with two sides, as shown in the working demo:

**The Storefront (customer side)**
- Drop page connected to your Square account
- Inventory that counts down live and can never oversell — sold out means the buy button turns off instantly
- Per-customer bag caps (default 2 per coffee)
- Waitlist signup, and wave announcements: scheduled texts that release the drop to your list in groups instead of all at once

**Drop Control (your side)**
- The daily roast sheet: orders are automatically added up, divided into 21-bag roasts, and scheduled onto roast days (max 24 roasts/day, adjustable to your real number)
- Drop manager: set the drop, the quantities, the caps, the waves
- Fulfillment: pick lists in roast order, thermal labels pre-printed with the declared roast date, postage purchased in one click
- Customer and waitlist lists

**Launch package:** setup of your Square connection, importing your customer list and waitlists, your label printer configured, and a working session where we run your first drop together.

*Not included in this agreement:* the AI inbox assistant (drafted replies to customer texts/emails). That is a Phase 3 add-on we can price separately once the system above is running.

## 4. Late payment and suspension

If any payment — build installment or monthly service fee — is **30 days past due**, Ogami may **suspend the system** (storefront, texts, roast sheet, and labels go offline) until the account is brought current.

- Your data (customers, orders, waitlists) is **never deleted** during suspension. It is preserved for at least 90 days.
- Service is reinstated within 1 business day of the account being brought current. No reinstatement fee.
- Under Option B, the system is licensed, not owned, until the build fee is fully paid.

## 5. Term and cancellation

- The monthly service continues month-to-month after launch. Either party may cancel with **30 days written notice**.
- On cancellation, Ogami provides a complete export of your data (customers, orders, waitlists) in a standard format within 14 days. Your data is yours.
- Ogami retains ownership of the software platform. S&W owns all of its business data, customer relationships, and content.

## 6. Timeline and changes

- The scope in Section 1 is fixed. New features beyond it (including the Phase 3 inbox assistant) are quoted and agreed in writing before any work or billing.
